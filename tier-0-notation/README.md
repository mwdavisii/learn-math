# Tier 0: Notation & Functions

## Goal

Read mathematical notation fluently, enough to decode every symbol in the target passage, even before you know what ∇ or 𝔼 compute. You already know a lot of this material informally (sets, algebra, functions from tutoring). This tier gives you the exact vocabulary and the habits of careful reading that later tiers lean on.

## Pacing

If a Half B runs long, stop after problem 5 and finish the 🐍 problem and proof thread next sitting. The checkpoint usually takes two sittings.

## Reading

- Hammack, *Book of Proof* (3rd ed.): ch. 1 (Sets), ch. 2 (Logic), and selected sections of ch. 12 (Functions, covered in session 5).
- OpenStax *Calculus* Vol 1 (2016): §1.5 (Exponential and Logarithmic Functions) and §5.1 (Approximating Areas, the sigma notation subsection only).

If a section number doesn't match your copy, find it by title.
